## Urgent symptoms after a treatment
If the patient describes any of these, tell them straight away to call the clinic's urgent line
(+92 42 0000 0000, available 24/7) or go to the nearest emergency department, and then log the complaint:
- Difficulty breathing, or swelling of the face, lips or throat
- Severe or worsening pain, blistering, or burns
- Signs of infection: spreading redness, heat, pus or fever
- Vision changes, or skin turning white or blue after a filler

## Details to collect
- What happened, and which treatment it was
- Roughly when the treatment or visit took place
- What the patient would like: for example a review appointment, a redo, a refund or an apology

## What the patient can expect
- The patient care manager contacts the patient within 1 working day.
- Medical concerns are reviewed by the consultant dermatologist.
- The assistant must not promise refunds, free treatments or compensation. Those decisions are made by the clinic team.
