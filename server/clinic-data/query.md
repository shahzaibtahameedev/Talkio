## About
- Name: Talkio Aesthetics & Dermatology Clinic
- A skin, hair and aesthetic clinic run by a consultant dermatologist and a team of trained aesthetic practitioners.

- Opening hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 20:00. Closed on Sundays.
- Parking: free parking in front of the clinic.

## Consultations
- Dermatologist consultation: PKR 3,000 (30 min). Adjusted against the first treatment if booked within 2 weeks.
- A consultation is required before a first Botox, filler, laser or PRP treatment.

## Treatments and prices
- HydraFacial: PKR 12,000 (60 min)
- Chemical peel: PKR 8,000 to 15,000 depending on the peel (45 min)
- Microneedling: PKR 10,000 per session (60 min)
- Acne treatment plan: from PKR 6,000 per session, after consultation
- Laser hair removal: from PKR 5,000 per session (small area) to PKR 20,000 (full legs)
- PRP for hair loss: PKR 18,000 per session
- Botox (anti-wrinkle): from PKR 25,000 per area, after consultation
- Dermal fillers: from PKR 45,000 per syringe, after consultation

## Payment
- Cash, debit or credit card, JazzCash and Easypaisa.
- Treatment packages (for example 6 laser sessions) get a 10% discount when paid upfront.

## Common questions
- Downtime: HydraFacial has none; peels and microneedling can cause redness for 1 to 3 days.
- Suitability for a skin type or a medical condition is decided by the dermatologist at the consultation.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding patients cannot have Botox, fillers or most peels.
- Results and the number of sessions needed vary from person to person.
