# Clinic information for the booking agent

## What to collect before booking
- The patient's name
- The treatment or consultation they want
- A date and a start time (free times always come from the checkSlots tool)

## Booking rules
- The clinic is open Monday to Saturday and closed on Sundays.
- First-time Botox, filler, laser or PRP patients must book a dermatologist consultation first. Offer to book the consultation instead.
- Chemical peels and laser hair removal need a patch test at least 48 hours before the first session. Mention this when booking them.
- One booking covers one treatment. For a second treatment, make a second booking.

## Before the appointment
- Chemical peel or microneedling: stop retinol and exfoliating products 3 days before.
- Laser hair removal: shave the area the day before; no waxing for 4 weeks before.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early. First-time patients fill in a short medical history form.

## Changes and cancellations
- Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before the appointment.
- Later cancellations or no-shows are charged PKR 1,000.
- To cancel, the patient needs their booking ID (for example B-1001).
